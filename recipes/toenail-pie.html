TOENAIL PIE

description

a delicious smelly pie featuring the ingrown toenails of your dad's diabetic feet

ingredients

  • 3/4 cup toenails of your dad (use nail cutters or pliers)
  • 2 cups cream cheese
  • 1 cup condensed milk
  • 1 tbsp. salt
  • 1 cup vinegar
  • 2x eggs
  • 4 cups flour
  • 2 cups fresh warm wet spit
  • 1 tsp. baking soda
  • sugar (to taste)
  • pepper (to taste)
  • secret ingredient: mom's special sauce
  1. mix all ingredients except toenails, cream cheese, and condensed milk and mom's special sauce
  2. knead the eventual dough
  3. put it in a tin pan and mold it so it looks like a pie crust, yk
  4. this time, mix toenails, cream cheese, and condensed milkand mom's special sauce
  5. put in crust
  6. set oven to 600 degrees celsius, careful not to set salad fingers on fire
  7. put pie in oven
  8. wait for pie to cook
